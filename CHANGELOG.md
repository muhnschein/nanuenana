# Changelog

## Unreleased

- First version: official warnings for Germany from warnung.bund.de, for saved places and, if you turn it on in *Settings*, for your current location.
- A place is a town, village or postcode with a distance of 1 to 25 km around it, or its whole district.
- Notifications for new warnings, set per category (civil protection, police, weather, flood) and level, for the end of a warning, and when checking has failed for 30 minutes.
- A place that could not be updated says so and never shows "No warnings".
- Warning texts are shown unchanged. Where the issuer provides a translation, *Settings › Language* chooses it.
- English and German.
