Nanünana
Version 0.1.0
An unofficial app that shows official warnings for places you choose and for where you are.
Not an official app
Volunteers make Nanünana. No authority runs, checks or endorses it. It can be late and it can miss a warning. In an emergency follow the official channels first.
Official channels in Germany
Cell broadcast on your phone, sirens, radio and television.
warnung.bund.de
Emergency calls: 112 (fire and rescue), 110 (police).
Where the warnings come from
Bundesamt für Bevölkerungsschutz und Katastrophenhilfe (warnung.bund.de), with warnings of the civil protection authorities of the Länder and districts, the police forces of the Länder, Deutscher Wetterdienst and the Länderübergreifendes Hochwasserportal. Texts are shown unchanged.
Place names: GeoNames, CC BY 4.0.
Where Nanünana is offered
Germany. More countries follow one by one.
Nanünana is not offered in the Russian Federation, in Belarus, or in territories under Russian control outside Russia. It is not offered in Iran, North Korea or Syria.
Your data
Places, settings and your position stay on this phone. Nanünana asks warnung.bund.de for the warnings of your districts; that service sees the district, not the place or your position. No account, no tracking.
