Distance, 1 to 25 km
You get every warning whose area comes within 5 km of the centre of Rehlingen-Siersburg. Warnings for the whole district or the whole Land always count.
A larger distance means more warnings that do not concern you. A smaller one can miss a warning drawn tightly around a neighbouring street.
